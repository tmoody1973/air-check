Air Check

Producer for Rhythm Lab Radio

Every show, ready to post. Nothing goes out without you.

Air Check reads the tracklist, the show notes and the audio, drafts the Mixcloud package, and waits for your approval.

  • 01The agent runs in a sandbox that blocks every network request not on its allow-list.
  • 02It never holds your Box, Drive, Dropbox or Mixcloud credentials.
  • 03Every request it makes is logged where you can read it.
