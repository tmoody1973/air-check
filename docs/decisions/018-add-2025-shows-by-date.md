# 018: Add a 2025 show to the backlog by date

**Decision.** The backlog stays 2026 by default. Tarik can add a single 2025 Friday on the Episodes page; only the dates he adds are scanned, drafted and posted like a 2026 show.

**Why this came up.** Some 2025 weeks never reached Mixcloud. The scanner, Convex and the Episodes date pickers all refused anything before 2026, so there was no way to post them. Most 2025 weeks are already on Mixcloud, so a careless widening would draft shows that are already up.

**Options.**
- **A. Add by date.** Tarik picks each 2025 Friday he wants. Before saving, the console checks Mixcloud's public show list (no login, read only) and warns if that date already has a post. Cost: one click per show, and a small new table of added dates.
- **B. Open all of 2025.** Every 2025 folder and doc joins the backlog. Cost: about 50 extra rows, most of them already posted, and a real risk of drafting a duplicate.
- **C. Open 2025 and hide what Mixcloud already has.** Automatic. Cost: Air Check would depend on reading show dates out of Mixcloud names (they come in several shapes), so one odd name means a duplicate draft.

**What we chose and why.** A, Tarik's call. It changes nothing for 2026, the duplicate check is a warning he can override, and removing a date (before it is drafted) undoes it.

**What we gave up.** It is manual: a week Tarik forgets is never offered. Blank-Friday gap rows stay 2026 only, so Episodes won't point out a missing 2025 week. A removed date whose show has a correction keeps its row (prune keeps corrected rows).

**How we'll know if this was right.** The 2025 gap weeks Tarik adds reach Mixcloud, and no 2025 show is posted twice.
