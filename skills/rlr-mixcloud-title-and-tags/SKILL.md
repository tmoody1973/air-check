# Rhythm Lab Radio: Mixcloud title and tags

Derived from the 100 most recent cloudcasts on the `rhythmlab` Mixcloud account (Jan 2022 to the 10.24.25 episode). These are starting rules; update them when Tarik edits a draft.

## Title pattern
`RLR M.D.YY | Artist, Artist, Artist, Artist and more`

- Date format is month.day.two-digit-year with dots (`10.3.25`).
- Prefix is `RLR`. Older posts also used `RL` and `Rl`; do not copy those.
- Separator after the date is `|` or `:`. Both appear in recent posts. Default to `|`.
- List 4 to 6 lead artists, comma separated, then `and more` when the show has more.
- Keep the total under 100 characters (Mixcloud's title limit as I understand it; verify on first upload).
- Use the exact spelling and diacritics from the Paper show notes.

## Choosing lead artists (starting heuristic, to be learned)
- Pull from both hours, not just hour 1.
- Include at least one widely recognized name and at least one newer or lesser-known artist.
- Prefer artists the show notes give a headline "Angle" or a thesis role.
- Do not pick artists whose entries carry an unresolved `VERIFY` flag if another artist works.
- Propose two alternates. Tarik's pick teaches the agent; record which was chosen and why if he says.

## Tags
- Mixcloud shows at most 5 tags per cloudcast (72 of 100 recent posts have exactly 5).
- Most-used tags in the archive: Jazz (84), Hip Hop (79), Electronic (74), R&B (70), Indie (41), Soul (15), Experimental (7), Afrobeats / Afropop (5).
- Pick 5 that describe the show's two hours. Default set is Jazz, Electronic, R&B, Hip Hop, plus one of Soul or Indie.
- Add a regional tag (Afrobeats / Afropop, Brazilian Jazz, and similar) only when a clear block of the show warrants it, and replace the weakest default tag rather than going over 5.

## Never
- Do not put release dates, "out today", or other time-relative phrases in a title.
- Do not invent artists or titles not in the tracklist.
