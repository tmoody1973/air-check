# Rhythm Lab Radio: public description rules

The Paper show notes are private prep: they mix facts, on-air angles, and items still to verify. The Mixcloud description is public and permanent. Convert one into the other with these rules.

## Structure
1. One line: "Rhythm Lab Radio, week of <Month D, YYYY>, hosted by Tarik Moody and produced by Radio Milwaukee."
2. One short paragraph per hour, built from each hour's "Spine of the Hour" section. Name 4 to 8 artists per hour. Keep it descriptive, not promotional.
3. Keep the whole description **under 1,000 characters** (Mixcloud's limit). Count before submitting; trim paragraph 2 first.
4. Do not paste the tracklist into the description. A 31-track list does not fit. The tracklist goes in Mixcloud `sections` (artist and song only; Tarik does not use start times). The Rhythm Lab web app sends sections the same way; confirm with an unlisted test upload that Mixcloud accepts that. If sections cannot be used, say so in the review flags rather than squeezing a partial list into the description.

## Leave out
- Anything under a `VERIFY` flag or in a "Pre-Air Verify List", unless it has since been confirmed by a source the agent can cite.
- Time-relative phrases: "out today", "out this week", "next Friday", "coming October 23". Use no dates for future releases at all.
- Personal health, family, legal, or other private details about artists, even when they appear in the show notes. Notes marked "handle with care on-air" are a strong signal. When unsure, leave it out and mention it in the review flags.
- Talk-break scripting and "Angle" or "Transition" lines. Those are on-air prep, not description copy.
- Anything the agent cannot trace to the notes or the tracklist.

## Tracklist formatting
- Order from the Drive tracklist; spelling and diacritics from the Paper notes.
- `Artist - "Title"` with `feat.` credits preserved.
- Use the pre-air tracklist only after it is reconciled (see rlr-source-reconciliation).

## Review flags
Every draft ends with a short list of what was left out and why, so Tarik can override. Example: "Left out Kiwanuka health note (marked handle with care)."

## Never publish
The agent drafts. A human approves. The publisher only accepts an approved package.
